# Apex Process Solutions — PMO Risk Management Policy (Summary)

**Document type:** Fictional company policy (continuity with Module 4/5 prompt sets)
**Source:** Self-created fictional example for classroom use — no real company, client, or
confidential information

## Scope

This policy applies to all active projects managed by the Apex Process Solutions
PMO. It defines how project risks are logged, rated, and escalated.

## Risk rating

Every logged risk must have a probability (Low / Medium / High / Certain) and an
impact (Low / Medium / High), rated using the Risk Probability × Impact
Prioritization Framework (see separate reference document). Risks are not rated
using a single combined "severity" number — probability and impact must be stated
separately so a reader can see which factor is driving the priority.

## Escalation rule

A risk must be escalated to the weekly PMO steering review when **any** of the
following is true:

1. Probability is Certain or High **and** impact is High.
2. The risk affects the project's ability to meet a client-committed date.
3. The risk depends on a decision or resource outside the project team's control
   (a dependency, in RAID-log terms) and no response has been logged within five
   business days of being raised.

## Data handling

Risk log entries must not contain client financial account numbers, personally
identifiable client contact information beyond a role name, or details of unrelated
client engagements. Entries should describe the risk in operational terms (schedule,
scope, resourcing, vendor, technical) rather than naming individual client
employees.

## Why this matters for AI-assisted PMO tools

An AI assistant that drafts or reviews risk log entries needs to check entries
against the escalation rule above — a rule the model would not know unless it is
retrieved from this document, since it is specific to this fictional organization's
own governance process, not general PM knowledge.

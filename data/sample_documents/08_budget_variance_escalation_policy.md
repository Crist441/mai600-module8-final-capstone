# Apex Process Solutions — Budget Variance Escalation Policy

**Document type:** Fictional company policy (continuity with Modules 4–7)
**Source:** Self-created fictional example for classroom use — no real company, client, or
confidential information

## Purpose

This policy defines when a project's spending must be escalated to the PMO steering
review because it has drifted from the approved budget baseline.

## Variance thresholds

Budget variance is measured as the percentage difference between actual-plus-committed
spend and the approved budget baseline, checked at each monthly close:

- **Under 5% variance:** No escalation required. Log the variance in the monthly status
  update for visibility only.
- **5%–10% variance:** The PM must document the cause (scope, vendor, resourcing, or
  estimation error) and a recovery plan, but escalation to steering is optional at the
  PM's discretion.
- **Over 10% variance, or any variance that will cause the project to miss its approved
  total budget by go-live:** Escalation to the weekly PMO steering review is mandatory,
  regardless of the cause.

## Relationship to vendor risk

Budget variance caused by a vendor or contractor issue must also be cross-checked against
the Vendor and Contractor Risk Addendum: the underlying vendor risk should already be
logged in the RAID log before the cost impact is reported here. A budget variance write-up
that does not reference an already-logged RAID entry (when the cause is vendor-related) is
considered incomplete.

## Example

*"A vendor delay is adding an estimated 12% to the integration workstream's budget due to
contractor overtime."* This crosses the mandatory 10% threshold, so escalation is required
— and because the cause is vendor-related, the escalation note must also reference the
corresponding RAID log risk entry for the vendor delay.

## Why this matters for AI-assisted PMO tools

This policy is frequently relevant together with vendor risk, since vendor delays are a
common cause of budget variance. A model answering a question about vendor-caused cost
overruns needs to retrieve both this document and the vendor risk addendum to give a
complete, non-contradictory answer.
